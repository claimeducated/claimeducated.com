Proprietary Framework

The CLAIM™ System

Five phases. One proven process. Total clarity on your property claim.

C
Capture

Document all damage thoroughly with photos, videos, and written records before anything is touched.

L
Log

Track every conversation, visit, and correspondence with dates, names, and outcomes.

A
Analyze

Read your policy with purpose. Know your coverages, limits, deductibles, and exclusions.

I
Inquire

Ask the right questions. Request itemized estimates. Challenge anything that doesn't add up.

M
Manage

Stay organized and follow through. A managed claim closes faster and more completely.
