Our Story

Built from the inside out.

ClaimEducated™ was founded on a simple truth: the people who need insurance most are also the least prepared to use it.

The Why Behind ClaimEducated™

An education gap that costs policyholders every single day.

Most people purchase homeowners or renters insurance and hope they never have to use it. But when disaster strikes — a burst pipe, a hailstorm, a fire — they find themselves navigating one of the most complex, documentation-heavy, and emotionally exhausting processes of their lives. Alone.

ClaimEducated™ exists to change that dynamic. Built on deep knowledge of how the claims process actually works from the inside, this platform gives policyholders the education, tools, and confidence to navigate their claims more strategically — without feeling overwhelmed, intimidated, or unprepared.

This is not about fighting your insurance company. It's about understanding your own policy, documenting your own claim, and knowing what to ask so nothing gets left on the table.

What ClaimEducated™ Stands For

◆
Empowerment Over Outrage
