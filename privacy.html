Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

1. Who We Are

ClaimEducated™ ("we," "our," or "us") is an educational brand that provides insurance claims education resources for policyholders. We are not a law firm, public adjusting firm, or insurance company. Our website is located at claimeducated.com.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Email addresses when you download a free resource or subscribe to our email list via Beehiiv.
  • Contact form submissions including your name, email, and message when you reach out through our contact page.
  • Purchase information handled entirely by Payhip and Amazon. We do not store payment data.
  • Usage data including anonymous analytics about how visitors use our site via Cloudflare Web Analytics, which does not use cookies.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

  • Deliver requested free resources via email
  • Send educational content and product updates (you can unsubscribe at any time)
  • Respond to your questions and messages
  • Improve our website and content

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following trusted third-party platforms. Please review their privacy policies for details on how they handle your data:

  • Beehiiv — email marketing and resource delivery
  • Payhip — digital product sales and delivery
  • Amazon KDP — print book sales
  • Calendly — scheduling free Q&A calls
  • Cloudflare — website hosting, DNS, and security

5. Cookies

Our website itself does not use tracking cookies. Third-party services embedded on our site such as Beehiiv forms or Payhip checkout may set their own cookies as governed by their respective privacy policies.

6. Your Rights

You may request to access, update, or delete your personal information at any time by emailing us at support@claimeducated.com. To unsubscribe from email communications, click the unsubscribe link in any email we send.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We encourage you to use caution when sharing personal information online.

8. Children's Privacy

Our website is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Continued use of our site constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email us at support@claimeducated.com.
