Common Questions

Before you write, check here

Do you represent policyholders?

No. ClaimEducated™ is an education platform, not a licensed public adjusting firm. We help you understand — you stay in control. For complex claims needing licensed representation, we can refer you.

What's covered in the free Q&A call?

Your specific claim question. Whether it's about documentation, adjuster communication, supplements, or a denial — bring your question and we'll point you in the right direction.

I bought a product and can't download it.

Check your spam folder first — Payhip/Gumroad download emails sometimes get filtered. If you still can't find it, send us a message with your order info and we'll get it to you.
